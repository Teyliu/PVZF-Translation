<align=center><size=130%>Plants VS. Zombies: Fusion - Update 3.8 Changelog</size></align>

1. The Gods: Evolved - Revolutionized Plus
- Added even more evolution paths and upgrade modifiers
- Added The Gods Expert plants

2. Zen Garden Update
- Added Page Skip function for fast navigation
- Added Background Change function, you can now adjust the ambience and lighting in the garden
- Added Endoflame Coins, selling fully grown and max favorability plants gives Endoflame Coins which can be used to buy bulk tools from the new Tool Shop
- Zen Garden and Grand Garden are now fully separate, you can no longer move plants between them

  - Doom-spreader (Doom-shroom + Threepeater)
  - Starry Squash (Squash + Starfruit)

5. Balance Adjustments
- Shroomageddon Modifier Rework: Blast Absorption > Volatile Barrier, now immune to all damage with a tradeoff
- Cosmo-pult & Astro-nut Modifiers & Mechanics Rework
- Infernal Squashwood & Infernal Sparkwood: Can now ignite more kinds of projectiles
- Element-infused Gargantuar Modifier Rework: Affinity Awakening now summons Element-infused Imps

6. QOL Optimizations
- New function to show projectile damage overlay on pressing T (toggleable in the settings)
- New UI position customization settings
- New View All Modifiers screen which allows viewing current modifiers in the Modifier Almanac
