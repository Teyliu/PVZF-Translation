<align=center><size=130%>Plants VS. Zombies: Fusion - Update 3.9 Changelog</size></align>

1. The Gods: Evolved
- Added a new Purgatory Mode
- Added new Trial Modifiers. These start off as debuffs but after completing the trials they turn into buffs.
- Access from Recommended Levels

  - Golden Hypno-shroom
  - Cherry Pop (Cherry Bomb > Puff-shroom)
  - Hungry-shroom (Chomper + Scaredy-shroom)
  - Bush-shroom (Fume-shroom + Umbrella Leaf)
  - Doom Pot (Doom-shroom > Flower Pot)
  - Doombrella (Doom-shroom + Umbrella Leaf)
  - Gulper-shroom (Chomper > Sea-shroom)
  - Winter Starfruit (Ice-shroom + Starfruit)
  - Belch-shroom (Chomper + Fume-shroom)
  - Nibble-shroom (Chomper > Puff-shroom)
  - Sea Pot (Sea-shroom > Flower Pot)
  - Starlic (Starfruit + Garlic)

  - Common:

  - Unique:

- Common:
  - Pole Vaulting Football Zombie
  - Nyan Squash Zombie
  - Boss:
    - Last Striker (finished)
    - Jackson Universe

4. Modifiers
- 6 new Zombie Modifiers

5. New Skin
- Obsidian Barrier Reef skin

6. Noteworthy Balance Changes
- Snow mechanic rework: Vanilla base plants now cost 75 more Sun on Snow maps, see Mechanics almanac for details. Also check out updated entries for Firnace, Frost Legion Snowball Launcher & Trident Thrower
- Fallout Blover: No longer deals double damage to Irradiated zombies
- Doom Cactus: Projectiles now pierce twice and inflict Irradiated
- Doom-shroom explosions now leave a Doom Pad on water tiles, see Lily Pad's entry for detailed explanations
- Doomwarp-shroom & Fungularity: Nerfed Chronoshift duration, but massively increased Chronoshift effect (by means of increasing elapsed time) to zombies already in Chronoshift
- SP Fusions: Now require dedicated Unlock Modifiers after obtaining the 2 corresponding Modifiers to unlock
- Epic Odyssey Fusion Unlock Modifiers are now more expensive in Cursed Odyssey
- Curse of Pride is now a Cursed Odyssey shop exclusive Modifier and no longer a Zombie Modifier

7. QOL Optimizations
- New function to show damage indicators (toggleable in the settings)
- New option in settings to disable screen shake effects (from Jalapenos & Doom-shrooms)
- New game speed settings that can be more fine-tuned
- New Insta-grow Fertilizer in Zen Garden Tool Shop, instantly maxes out a Plant

8. Miscellaneous bug fixes and optimizations.
