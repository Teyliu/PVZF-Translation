<align=center><size=130%>Plants VS. Zombies: Fusion - Update 3.7 Changelog</size></align>

1. The Gods: Evolved - Revolutionized
- Added many gameplay customization options, including plant limits, difficulty levels, and progression tiers.
- Modifiers now come in multiple rarities and can be used to obtain new plants or upgrade existing ones.
- Be on the look out for special modifiers handed out by the gods, they can be game-breaking! 
  * (Note: You can access this level through the new "Recommended Levels" button on the main menu.)

4. Odyssey Update
- Added Archduke Deathrider's route.
- Adjusted spawns for existing routes.
- Added the possibility to gain Diamond modifiers from The Gods: Evolved in regular Odyssey runs.
- Added Napalm-shroom + Doominator synergy modifier.
- Added pets:
  - Young Duke (Archduke Deathrider)
  - Imperor Hatchling (Imperor's Ark of the Duck)
  - Skyglider Mecha (Skystrider Mecha)

5. New Levels
- Mini-games:
  - Odyssey Gacha: Gacha Box
  - Wheelbarrow Challenge
  * (Note: Completing this mini-game will unlock the new Wheelbarrow tool permanently.)
  - Time Attack

6. Balance Adjustments
- Torchweed can now ignite mini-nut projectiles.
- Reduced the speed of Chicken Jockey and Hydrofowl zombies.
- Reduced the amount of metal objects produced by Dyson Magnet while increasing its potential damage boost.
- The "Curse" mechanics (held by Archduke Deathrider and Lifelink Ladder Zombie) have been fully reworked.
- Celestial Gargantuar now only does one full-screen attack right after becoming giant.

7. QOL Optimizations
- If you are not on the latest version of the game, an update option will become available on the main menu. (Requires an internet connection to work)
<color=red>* (WARNING: This has NOT been tested or verified to work with the Blooms translation mod.)</color>
- Added a Modifier Almanac section.
- Reworked plant HP display.
- Reworked the Survival save system, including more save slots and custom naming support.
- Added the ability to preview the full lineup of zombies in a level before starting.
- Zombies can no longer move forward in between attacks if they still have to attack a plant.
- Added a variable system to the Node Editor in the Super Level Editor.
- Mini-games with the "Blessings and Curses" system now have a difficulty selection menu.
- Tower Defense is now located in the Challenge menu.
