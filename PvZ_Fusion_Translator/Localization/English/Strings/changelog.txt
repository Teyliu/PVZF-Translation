6. Noteworthy Balance Changes
- Firnace: buffed Sun production and duration, increased seed packet CD, to reduce micro-management
- Doom Kelp: No longer leaves a Doom Lily Pad on explosion
- Charm-shroom & Charmatron: buffed attack speed, nerfed attack damage
- Both Magnetar Synergy Modifiers [Astral Convergence], [Celestial Beacon] reworked
- Pulsar: Now Magnetar Modifiers also have effects for Pulsar
- Coach Zombie: Pole-vaulting distance buff no longer stacks
- Professor Cherryz: Now more threatening the longer he remains on the field
