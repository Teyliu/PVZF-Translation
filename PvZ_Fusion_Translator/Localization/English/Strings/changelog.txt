<align=center><size=130%>Plants VS. Zombies: Fusion - Update 4.1 Changelog</size></align>
1. Odyssey: Evolved OVERHAULED
- Earn points by clearing rounds; use these points to purchase plants, upgrade, or refresh the shop.
- Synthesize plants or upgrade the shop to obtain higher-quality cards.
- Equip plant cards with various items to gain additional bonuses.
- When plants on the field are fused, the resulting plant inherits the quality and equipment of the materials used.
- Zombies reaching your house no longer cause an immediate level failure; instead, you lose a portion of your health.

6. Balance Adjustments
- Blover Pot, Levitater, Solar Pumpkin: Stats buffed.
- Threepeater Series: Attack speed nerfed.
- Metallic Plants: Significantly increased healing when consuming the corresponding metal items.
- Magnet Blover: Converts consumed metal items into collectible drops.
- Calamity-shroom & Avarice-shroom: Effects reworked. Removed usage restrictions for Avarice-shroom in the "Cursed" Odyssey difficulty.
- Tridents: Now apply a slowing effect to targets with the "Drenched" status.
- Pozeidon: Bites deal equal damage to a trident and apply Drenched; the Giant Trident generates a vortex that draws in projectiles.
- Trident Jugger-nut Gargantuar: Throws trident upon being hit instead of reaching 50% hp or lower, refills tridents at 1 trident per second.

7. Other Optimizations
- In Gacha Modes, the next wave will no longer spawn immediately if the total HP of zombies currently on the field is at or under half.
- Optimized facial expression animations for certain plants.
- The "Magic Brush" (Image Recognition) plugin has been made server-side, eliminating dependency on the user's local device.
- Optimized the pacing of score accumulation and modifier ​​triggers in Classic Odyssey Mode.

8. Fixed known bugs and improved the overall gaming experience.
- Fixed a bug where the Mecha Fighter would vanish after a scene transition in The Gods: Evolution.
- Fixed a bug where the "Investing" modifier was incorrectly receiving score multipliers from the Cursed Odyssey difficulty setting.
