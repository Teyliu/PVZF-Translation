⑥バランス調整
 ※Torchweed can now ignite mini-nut projectiles.
 ※Reduced the speed of Chicken Jockey and Hydrofowl zombies.
 ※Reduced the amount of metal objects produced by Dyson Magnet while increasing its potential damage boost.
 ※The "Curse" mechanics (held by Archduke Deathrider and Lifelink Ladder Zombie) have been fully reworked.
 ※Celestial Gargantuar now only does one full-screen attack right after becoming giant.
